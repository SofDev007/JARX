JARX 2.1.6 (build 44)

A required update: Jarvix lights up your screen when you open it.

In short
- This update is required: songs play again once it's installed
- Tap the Jarvix blob: the edges of your screen light up for 5 seconds as the chat opens
- Update notices are shorter and easier to read

What's new
- Opening the Jarvix chat (tapping the blob, or Settings › Jarvix › Talk to Jarvix) lights up the edges of your screen for 5 seconds, the same glow as turning Jarvix on.
- Update notices (the pop-up, the bell and Settings › Check for updates) show only the short version of what's new. The full notes stay here on GitHub.
- Fixed: a phone on an earlier Jarvix version (like 2.1.2) wasn't told when an update was required, and its bell could miss the update. JARX now compares the whole version, not only the build number.
- JARX now checks for updates at the project's new GitHub address.
- Plugin Defaults is simpler: the Search Suggestions choice moved out of it.
