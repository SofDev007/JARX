JARX 2.2.7 (build 52)

A required update: songs you queue always play next, and the player has a cleaner look.

In short
- This update is required: songs play again once it's installed
- Songs you add to the queue always play next, in the order you added them
- New player layout: a Queue button, Repeat in the main row, and a bar that shows where the music is playing
- New: ask Jarvix "how do I…" and it shows you a short video; Report a problem in Settings
- Smart shuffle mixes in a new song after every 2 of yours

What's new
- Jarvix how-to videos: ask Jarvix how to download a song, download only on Wi-Fi, bring a Spotify playlist, watch the music video, stop a song being suggested or switch account (in English or Hinglish), and it answers with the steps and a "Watch how" video. It also tells you how to update JARX and report a problem.
- Report a problem: Settings › Report a problem. Write what went wrong and tap Send; it goes straight to the developer with your JARX version and phone model.
- Queue: songs you add with "Add to queue" now always play right after the current song, first in, first out, ahead of the rest of the playlist, Smart shuffle's picks and suggestions. Queuing a song that's already further down moves it up (no double). Turning shuffle on keeps your queued songs next.
- Player: the Queue button (top right) opens Up Next; swiping up still works. Repeat sits in the main row next to Previous. The sleep timer moved to the top left, next to the close button, and "Enjoying from" sits in the middle. The lyrics button is gone: tap the lyric line to open the lyrics.
- Player: a new bar at the bottom shows where the music is playing: this phone, or your Bluetooth or wired headphones. Tap it to see the device and switch speakers or headphones.
- Jarvix chat: every message shows when it was sent ("10:42 pm", "Yesterday, 10:42 pm", "21 Nov, 10:42 pm").
- Smart shuffle: a suggestion now comes after every 2 songs from your playlist (it was every 3), it works again after you've used normal shuffle, it keeps looking until it finds enough songs, and the first suggestions show up within a few seconds (it starts looking the moment you tap, and adds them as they're found). Tapping + on a suggestion in Up Next shows the song in the playlist right away. Its button and its songs in Up Next show the Smart shuffle icon.
- Tablets: the Jarvix blob now shows in the full-screen tablet layout too.
- Jarvix blob: you can hold it and then drag it to the other side (before, holding opened "Nap for today" and the blob wouldn't move). Hold and let go without moving still opens the nap menu.
- Tips: people who already use JARX no longer see the beginner tips again. New users still get the full tour. "End tour" is now "Don't show tips".
- New tip: tap the JARX logo on Home to meet the developer.
- Fixed: "Failed to open part file: No such file or directory" when opening JARX. If your phone's cleaner (or Clear cache) emptied JARX's temporary folder, waiting downloads failed every time; they now start again normally.
- Required updates: if a required update is waiting and you press play, JARX tells you why the song didn't start, with a "Click here" that opens the bell.
