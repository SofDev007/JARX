JARX 2.1.5 (build 43)

Meet Jarvix (Just A Rather Vibe Intelligent eXpert), your music buddy.

In short
- Jarvix: tap the glowing blob and ask for a mood, any song, its video, or how to do something in JARX
- A quick tour shows you what's new
- Settings › Jarvix to turn it on or off
- Hold your photo on Home to switch account

What's new
- Jarvix: the glowing blob at the bottom of Home and Library. Tap it and tell it how you feel, or ask for anything ("hype me up", "old is gold", "roast my taste"). It answers in your language (Hinglish too) and makes a mix mostly from your own playlists, played from YT Music. Tap 🎬 on any song in a mix to watch its video.
- The blob's colour follows the mood of the song playing. It pulses with the music, thinks with a wobble, and closes its eyes when you're offline. Drag it to the other side, or hold it to let it nap for today.
- Jarvix is off until you turn it on. Your name never leaves your phone, and your chats stay on your phone only. See what it remembers about you (and edit or delete it) in its ⋮ menu. Hold a reply to copy or report it.
- Smarter moods: every song now gets real mood tags (sad, romantic, dance, calm...), so autoplay and Smart shuffle keep the vibe better.
- Long-press your photo on Home to switch account.
- A quick tour of JARX: tips point at what's new, one after another (tap Next), and you can end the tour any time. A few must-knows still show after that: swipe up on the player for the queue, swipe down to tuck it away, ⊖ to never see a song again, and YouTube Video's music videos.
- Settings › Jarvix: turn Jarvix on or off, let it nap for today, see what it remembers, clear the chat, and read what it can do. Turning it on lights up the edges of your screen.
- Ask Jarvix how to do anything in JARX ("how do I download songs?", "how do I sync lyrics?") and it walks you through it, step by step.
- Ask Jarvix to play a song ("play Kesariya", "Tum Hi Ho bajao") and it plays right away: from your own playlist when the song is in one, otherwise from YT Music.
- Jarvix's mixes find their songs faster and show them as they're found, with Try again if YT Music can't be reached.
- 🎬 on a song opens its music video from YouTube Video.
- The chat opens on your latest messages and scrolls smoothly.
- Ask for a music video ("play the video of Aadat", "Kesariya ka video dikhao") and Jarvix plays it from YouTube Video, straight into the video player.
- The "Finding the video" message now goes away as soon as the video starts.
- Mix buttons (Play, Shuffle, Save) no longer break their words on small screens.
- Fixed: after switching account, Jarvix showed the previous account's chat. Each account now has its own Jarvix chat and memory.
- Fixed: Jarvix sometimes answered with just 🎶, especially when you asked the same thing twice.
